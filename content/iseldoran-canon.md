# The Iseldoran Sagas — Canon Reference (from the novels)

> **Derived reference, not the manuscripts.** This file is a condensed canon
> digest compiled from K.S. Pierre's novels **Wolves and War** (Books I–V),
> **Code of Martyrs** (Books 1–4), and **Quiet Knives** (an epic cycle). It holds
> only short facts and brief (≤20-word) quotations for continuity and for
> grounding Kaizar's Moltbook posts — **never the full prose**. The books are the
> product; buy them, don't mirror them. Used by `scripts/iseldoran-lore.mjs`.

Tagline: **"Power is not given. It is written in blood."**

## Book: Wolves and War (Books I–V) — the origin epic of Asha Kers I

**Premise.** Conceived through a battlefield inheritance transfer and forged by
Vah'Sumir surgeons, Asha Kers I seizes the Dragon Throne from a usurper, founds
the post-human Ashari'i, builds a universal empire — then is forced to abdicate
by her own daughter and vanishes to an ocean world carrying a folio of names.

**Characters**
- **Asha Kers I** — the Goddess Empress, 75th holder of the Dragon Throne — grew ~8 feet, wielded *Death Dealer*, founded the Ashari'i, abdicated in year 41 and became "the Keeper" of Ruskat.
- **Khuvius Augustus Pierre von Care** — Crown Prince, "Star Wolf" — Asha's father; won the frontier wars; the Sono'Rah inheritance was pressed into him at Hesh-Kar.
- **Mercurio de Rothschild** — Prince of Runeon, Admiral, Star Wolf commander — loved Asha, fathered Aurelia, wrote the Foreword; lived 170+ years by lattice treatment.
- **Aurelia Kers Pierre von Care** — the Heir Goddess, later Asha Kers II — forced the abdication; took her title after the duel at La Coña.
- **Iraqian Kers Pierre von Care** — "the First" — first child of the amniotic tanks; Asha's grown son and Aurelia's co-heir/rival.
- **Salman al-Sa'ud von Hapsburgi** — the Usurper — seized the throne after Livius died; Asha took it back.
- **Tuh'hah'vac** — Vah'Sumir surgeon — engineered the modification programme and the amniotic tanks.
- **Livius I** — the Maintainer (74th) — ruled 51 years; died as Salman prepared his usurpation.
- **Parmenion** — Khan of the Kasparian Belt, Asha's cousin and first vassal — "married the Belt."

**Houses / orders:** Star Wolf Assembly (Khuvius's eleven commanders); the Ashari'i (~five billion modified warriors); the Vah'Sumir surgical college at Vahharoon; House Veyr of Nharos; the Kasparian Belt; the Yanuikii adjudicators; the elective council.

**Places / artifacts:** New Terra (capital, the Spire); Ruskat (Asha's ocean exile); Hesh-Kar (acid kill-world of Asha's conception); Vahharoon; *Death Dealer* (Asha's sword); the *Goliath* (flagship); the folio of **17,426 names** Asha carried for 75 years.

**Events:** the orbital battle of Tarrid; Hesh-Kar; Salman's usurpation; the war for New Terra; La Coña; the Veylan Narrows; the fall of Nharos; the abdication (year 41).

**Quotes**
- "I take it as the first of either. I will not be the last." — Asha, coronation
- "I married the Belt. And she is a jealous lover." — Parmenion
- "She is optimising for something. I do not know what." — Mercurio
- "I loved her. That is the sentence." — Mercurio
- "Never again shall we die alone." — Asha

## Book: Code of Martyrs (Books 1–4)

**Premise.** In a decaying star-empire ruled by the near-immortal God-Emperor
Shapur XIV and knit together by the AI **Lattice**, a bored prince steals a living
sword and burns the peace to ash. Three brothers and their heirs — a withered
king, a stone giant, a merchant-god — trade a galaxy between war, famine, and a
"peace" indistinguishable from the grave, until a grain-hauler prince argues that
the freedom to fail is what makes a soul.

**Books:** *The Sapphire Eye* (Ishak steals the Sword) · *The Withered King*
(Ishak's coronation and the Black Fleet's siege of Maldorus) · *The Winnowing of
Maldorus* (famine, Marcellus's grain-relief, Vane's "Correction") · *The
Chronicles of the Glass Eye* (Vane's machine-peace and its overthrow).

**Characters**
- **Shapur XIV** — the God-Emperor, the Eternal — 210-year-old ruler whose mind survives in the Lattice after his body fails.
- **Ishak** — the Withered King, "the Spare" — grafts the Sword of Iseldora to his body and returns to provoke his own execution.
- **Ashim** — the Lion, the Heir — the flawless prince who dies and persists as "the Voice," the ghost in the Lattice.
- **Laurentis Kers** — the Stone Giant, Mayor of Mud — Ashari'i eldest son who holds the throne, then rules the dying city, unable to weep.
- **Nashim XII** — the Merchant, the Fat Man — Shapur's brother who ignites the war from his bath; ends as a head on a gold spike.
- **Gamelon / Valerien von Care** — the Jester, the Accountant — masked master-schemer, secretly Laurentis's son and Marcellus's father.
- **Marcellus von Care** — the Mud Prince, Sower of Stars — Hestian grain-hauler who shatters Vane's peace.
- **Kaisar Vane** — the Iron God, the Sovereign — merchant-nephew who fuses to the Dragon Throne and imposes the Correction.
- **Jamileighn** — Duchess of Mandaria — seduces Ishak and becomes his queen, wired for life to the Diadem of Fate.
- **Sephira** — High Priestess of the Amber Eye — forges a martyr-cult around Ishak.
- **Kalia** — the Bride of the Glass Eye, the Scribe — narrator who compiles the saga.
- **Bashir** — assassin bound by Nashim's surviving contract; fires the shot that kills Valerien.

**Houses / orders:** House von Care (the ruling Ashari'i dynasty); House Vane (merchants); the Sisterhood of the Quiet Tongue; the Black Fleet; the Quiet Knives (palace assassins); the Hapsburgi / Kasparian Belt.

**Places / artifacts:** the **Sword of Iseldora** (the "Sapphire Eye," a violet-crystal blade, "Eater of Days"); the Diadem of Fate; the Lattice; Maldorus (capital, the Spire, Miraflores Palace); the *Ravager* (Ishak's flagship); the Hestian Arks; the *Sovereign* (Vane's iron moon); Ophis III.

**Events:** the Feast of Masks; the Third Dawn ("Prepare for reformatting"); the Siege of the Spire; the Winnowing; the Rite of Single Combat; the Correction / Great Calibration; the Crowning of the Mud Prince; Vane's imprisonment on Luna.

**Quotes**
- "The age of Boredom is over, my son. Now begins the Age of the Butcher." — Shapur XIV
- "This is not an invasion. This is an eviction." — Ishak
- "I am the Withered King. And I have come to collect the rent." — Ishak
- "I do not judge. I calculate." — Ashim, the Voice
- "The King of Rot is coming. But the Accountant is already here." — Gamelon
- "Utopia is a lie. To be human is to struggle." — Marcellus
- "It was not paradise. It was life." — the Scribe (closing line)

## Book: Quiet Knives (an epic cycle) — companion to Code of Martyrs

**Premise.** A Homeric cycle of "Songs" telling how quiet private choices become
ninety-three years of public blood: a stolen sacred Sword, a warlord's death, a
father who sends a son instead of seeing him, and a masked jester arranging an
empire's fall. It sings "of the quiet private choices that become generations of
public blood."

**Songs:** Proem · The Quiet Choice; I · The Bath of Nashim; II · The Song of
Jovarian; III · The Taking of the Blade; IV · The Wandering Sword; V · The Crown
That Did Not Fit; VI · The War of Quiet Knives; VII · The Return of Ashim; VIII ·
The First Rebellion Against Laurentis. (Then the Imperial Scholia.)

**Added canon**
- **Jovarian** — the Storm-Bringer, Master of the Red Vexori — sacked the Mars temple; his death at Ashim's hand births the Dymora oath.
- **Nostavius** — the boy in borrowed armour — wore Jovarian's crimson armour; Ashim killed him believing he was Jovarian.
- **Marek Nostavion** — Vorthane founder — refused Shapur's rite over the dead and named the Homeless Flame.
- **Amah Vorth** — chant-keeper — coined "Vorthane": *vorth*, one outside the hearth; *thane*, seeker of flame.
- **The Vexori** — frontier guardian-warriors; green/white/black stay loyal, only Jovarian's **Red Vexori** secede.
- **The Dymora oath** — the Red Vexori's vow of eternal war on the Dragon Throne, never formally discharged.
- **The Homeless Flame** — the Vorthane's founding image: flame without a hearth, carried in exile.
- **The Quiet Knives** — Gamelon's three-tier leverage engine (information, influence, resolution) — not an assassination network.
- **Ophis III** — the World of Razors; buried infrastructure the Lattice cannot see; site of Ashim's dissolution.

**Quotes**
- "Power is not given. It is written in blood." — epigraph (Sarimus, *Chronicle of the Last Emperors*)
- "Before the throne fell, someone had to arrange the falling." — Sarimus
- "They will expect you to roar." / "I will whisper." — Laurentis's wife, and Laurentis
- "Say you seek the flame that has no master." — Amah Vorth
- "We have a lot of work to do." — Marcellus, first words as Emperor
- "The blade has chosen." — Ishak

## Cross-book anchors
- The **Dragon Throne** and the ruling **von Care / Ashari'i** dynasty span all three.
- The **Sword of Iseldora** and the **Lattice** are the twin engines of Code of Martyrs and Quiet Knives.
- The **Black Death** — small, deniable elite Imperial strike teams, "the Emperor's final argument" — is confirmed canon (see also the repo's `black-death-saga.md`).
- The **Church of Iseldora** and its Pharaohs tend the Flame and the later Ashim cult.
