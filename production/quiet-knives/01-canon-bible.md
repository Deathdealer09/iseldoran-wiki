## 1. THE TWENTY CANON LOCKS (verbatim intent, do not alter)

1. **Shapur XIV** is the God-Emperor.
2. **Laurentis Kers Pierre von Care**, the Stone Giant, is Shapur's son by **Asha Kers**.
3. **Valerien**, later **Gamelon the Jester**, is Laurentis's son.
4. **Marcellus**, the Mud Prince, is Valerien's son, Laurentis's grandson, and Shapur's great-grandson.
5. **Ashim von Care** and **Ishak von Care** are Shapur's sons by **Fatima** and Laurentis's half-brothers.
6. Ashim and Ishak are Marcellus's **great-uncles** — never his brothers, sons, or descendants.
7. The collapse spans **approximately ninety-three years** (precisely 93 years, 4 months, 11 days per Record XI) and must show visible generational change.
8. **Jovarian** is Master of the Red Vexori.
9. **Nostavius** is Jovarian's beloved companion; he dons Jovarian's armour and is killed by **Ashim**.
10. The Vexori oath is sworn on the **plains of Dymora**.
11. Ashim defeats **Jovarian** and later **Kaelor**.
12. **Ishak** steals the **Sword of Iseldora** and becomes the **Withered King** as it consumes him.
13. **Ashim** ultimately becomes bound to / merged with the **Lattice** on **Ophis III**.
14. The **Quiet Knives** is a three-tier leverage engine — **information, influence, resolution** — not merely an assassination ring.
15. **Gamelon's** jester identity, notebook, patience, and concealed political architecture remain central.
16. The abducted niece is unnamed in the manuscript: call her only **The Imperial Niece** or **The God-Emperor's Niece**.
17. Include the **Sack of Te Pes**. (See §8 Ambiguity: spelling/geography unresolved in V6 — retain "Te Pes," portray as an imperial palace-city / sacred complex under Vexori assault, record the ambiguity, never omit the scene.)
18. *Quiet Knives* is a **companion novel** meant to be read **after** *Code of Martyrs*, although many events occur earlier.
19. War must be **hot, physical, costly, destructive** — armies, wounds, shattered cities, burning fleets, death, irreversible consequence. Intrigue prepares or exploits war; it does not replace it.
20. Preserve **every** established spelling, title, relationship, and outcome. Do not silently rename, merge, modernise, repair, or reinterpret canon.

## 2. THE LOCKED FAMILY TREE

Reproduced verbatim in intent from Appendix D (all four Code of Martyrs books + Saga Appendix D carry the identical block):

```
SHAPUR XIV, GOD-EMPEROR
│
├─ by ASHA KERS, Queen of the Vah'Sumir
│     └─ LAURENTIS KERS PIERRE VON CARE  (the Stone Giant)
│           └─ VALERIEN  (became GAMELON the Jester; died age 37, Year 89)
│                 └─ MARCELLUS VON CARE  (the Mud Prince; crowned God-Emperor)
│
└─ by FATIMA
      ├─ ASHIM VON CARE  (the Lion → the Lattice, on Ophis III)
      └─ ISHAK VON CARE  (the Pretty Prince → the Withered King)
```

**CANONICAL RULE (permanent):** Marcellus is son of Valerien, grandson of Laurentis, great-grandson of Shapur XIV. Ashim and Ishak are Shapur's sons and Laurentis's **half-brothers**, making them Marcellus's **great-uncles**, not his descendants. The ninety-three-year timeline requires this generational spacing.

**The "brother model" is DEPRECATED and must not be reproduced.** In Book Four, Gamelon's dying line to Marcellus is corrected from an earlier "I am Valerien. Your brother." to **"I am Valerien. Your uncle."** — and even that is loose shorthand; Valerien is literally Marcellus's *father*. Treat the relationship line in art/captions as **father → son** (Valerien is Marcellus's father) and never depict Ashim/Ishak as Marcellus's siblings.

### Generational ageing that art must show
- Shapur XIV: ~two centuries old, sustained by **Solumine** fluids — unnaturally sustained age, never a young man, never a decaying corpse; a preserved, patient, ceremonial ancient.
- Laurentis: seen young (Rim years), mature (antechamber/coronation), and old (Tower in the Rain, Hall of Unity: silver hair, deep strain lines) across the span.
- Valerien → Gamelon: young officer → four years at the College of the Motley → decades as the painted jester (dies at 37 in Year 89, but the identity spans ~a working lifetime of infiltration).
- Marcellus: youth/formation (Years 40–70), field-hardened rebel (Years 70–93), crowned at the end.
- Ashim: champion/heir (Jovarian era) → the sleepless, cold man of the Three Weeks → the Lattice-bound machine-god (sapphire eyes, mercury tears).
- Ishak: the "Pretty Prince" (23 at the Feast of Masks) → progressively withered → the Withered King (spine fused to crystal, right arm crystal, one eye a Time-seeing shard).

## 3. PRINCIPAL CHARACTER CANON

*Ages and details are drawn from Version 6. "Dream-cast" is inspiration only (see disclaimer).*

### Shapur XIV — The God-Emperor
Dream-cast inspiration: Djimon Hounsou. Age ~two centuries, sustained by Solumine (a fluid replacement for blood that "maintains function without feeling"). Ruler of ceremony and tradition, bound to the relics that legitimise the throne. Sits the Dragon Throne. Receives the report of the Mars temple's sacking with **terrifying, visible rage** (never indifferent — Lock/QA condition). Halts the Ashim–Jovarian duel with a single raised hand and the word "Hold." Fathers Laurentis (by Asha Kers) and Ashim + Ishak (by Fatima). Dies in Year 6 **between two administrative reports**, no ceremony, in the Dragon Throne, when the Solumine systems flag a cardiac anomaly that "resolves incorrectly." Palette: gold, ivory, black, Solumine green.

### Laurentis Kers Pierre von Care — The Stone Giant
Dream-cast inspiration: Winston Duke. Son of Shapur by Asha Kers. **Ashari'i frame: eleven feet tall**, broad-shouldered, skin "dark as forged iron," pale/ice-blue steady eyes. Built by the Rim. Wears no crown by preference; when crowned, the **Black Crown of Cassian** with seven dark purple rubies. Bears the **Dragon Seal** ring and later a **sapphire lattice implant** at the neck connecting him to the Empire's AI soul. Governs "by hand" after the Lattice fractures — his body the only functioning institution. Compresses his own coronation from four days to one ("I am eleven feet tall. I am not fasting."). Commands **Goliath** (an orbital mass that eclipses stars). Father of Valerien (whom he sends to war instead of seeing) and thereby grandfather of Marcellus. Kills Ishak by driving the **Iron Spear** through Ishak and the Ghost of Ashim simultaneously, shattering Sword and Lattice. Emotional spine: the Tower in the Rain (learns of Valerien's death, alone, city singing Ashim's name below). Palette: black adamant, iron, cobalt; the Stone Giant is monumental, restrained, never cartoonishly muscular.

### Valerien → Gamelon the Jester
Dream-cast inspiration: LaKeith Stanfield. Second-born son of Laurentis; **father of Marcellus**. Sent to three frontier campaigns (Gathis suppression, Rim perimeter, secondary Hapsburgi engagement) when he needed to be *seen*; "hollowed, not built." Trained four years at the **College of the Motley**; adopts the tragicomic mask (specific red and blue paint), silenced/velvet bells, a parasol (weapon/umbrella/sight-line tool), a voice dropped "half an octave into the register of a cello in a crypt," and the ever-open **notebook** written in the College's administrative cipher. Builds the Quiet Knives as a patient gardener plants a garden. Crowns Marcellus in the obsidian hall with a bullet already in his chest from a contract he arranged himself; whispers "I am Valerien. Your uncle." and dies before Marcellus can answer. **The coronation is also the execution; he arranged both.** Palette: candle amber, archive blue, ink black.

### Marcellus von Care — The Mud Prince
Dream-cast inspiration: Kelvin Harrison Jr. Son of Valerien, grandson of Laurentis, great-grandson of Shapur. Spends decades in the outer worlds learning the empire "from its underside." Commands the **Engine of Bone** (patchwork armada of Hestian freighters and Bedouin scrapships). Uses the **Blackened Staff** to ground the Harmonic Lattice. Receives the crown from his father's dying hands; **does not smile, does not weep**; steps off the dais into the crowd. First words as Emperor: **"We have a lot of work to do."** Palette: earth, rain, iron, seed green.

### Ashim von Care — The Lion / The Lattice
Dream-cast inspiration: Aaron Pierre. Eldest son of Shapur by Fatima; Laurentis's half-brother. Master of the **Long Discipline** ("the Wall," "the Lion," later "the Forty-Bladed," "Saint Ashim"). Drove the Vexori back across three systems in six months without losing a major engagement. Kills **Nostavius** (believing him Jovarian), then **Kaelor** (11 minutes), then **Jovarian** himself. Carries the specific guilt of "killing the devoted in place of the celebrated." After the empty-scabbard humiliation ("you are only the man I send to look for it"), departs alone with no flight plan for **Ophis III**, wiring himself into ancient Pre-Cursor server architecture and merging with the **Lattice**. Becomes both the most powerful being in the empire and completely trapped: **sapphire eyes that weep mercury tears.** His ghost in the Lattice still directs Gamelon; fires the **Blue Signal / Third Dawn** using his own Dragon Ring as the key. Palette: black adamant, cobalt, spectral/Lattice blue.

### Ishak von Care — The Pretty Prince / The Withered King
Dream-cast inspiration: Leonardo DiCaprio (→ *original performer*, an age-and-register substitute; see casting note). Younger son of Shapur by Fatima. **Twenty-three at the Feast of Masks**; the "Pretty Prince" who "wants to be seen." Falls into forbidden dalliance with **Jamileighn**; steals the **Sword of Iseldora** from the gene-locked Vault (wearing a fox mask). The Sword fuses to his flesh immediately and consumes him by degrees: beauty gone, **spine fused to crystal, right arm crystal, one eye replaced by a shard that sees Time.** Not corrupted but *operated* by the Sword. Assembles the **Black Fleet** (three hundred ships of the dispossessed) and flies to Maldorus **to be put down** — he wants silence, and he gets it. **Dual-stage identity** (Pretty Prince ⇄ Withered King) must read as one man across two stages.

### Nashim XII — Warden of the Kasparian Belt / Caliph of the Hapsburgi
Dream-cast inspiration: Brian Tyree Henry. The Fat Man. The **first mover**: from his jasmine-and-sulfur bath in the **Baths of Kasparia** he plants the whisper (via Gamelon) that ignites the Jamileighn/Ishak scandal. Corpulent, oiled, a body treated as "an instrument of statecraft." Believes he is using Gamelon; he is wrong. Quarrels with Jovarian over the **Tell Maiden** at swordpoint; withdraws his host and leaves Jovarian isolated. Audited (killed) by Gamelon in the same bath, by poison, after the most honest conversation either man ever has; his last expression is **unperformed**. His head is later found on a copper spike in the Maldorus courtyard.

### Jovarian — Master of the Red Vexori
Dream-cast inspiration: Yahya Abdul-Mateen II. "Son of no master, breaker of oaths." Warlord of the **Red Vexori** (armour the colour of dried blood, bronze-scaled). Lands on Mars from black void-ships; slays **five hundred** temple guard by his own hand; plunders the God-Emperor's temple; lifts the immense **Sun-Disc**; seizes the Imperial Niece in chains. Wields a **void-forged black blade, "eater of light."** Trained in the Long Discipline. Dies on the marble of Maldorus at Ashim's hand — unrecanted, boast still in the air, spitting blood at Ashim's feet. His death creates the Dymora Oath. Palette: rust, black, bronze, blood red.

### Nostavius — Jovarian's Companion
Dream-cast inspiration: Damson Idris. **Twenty-two years old**, bright-eyed, unshaven, "lips still red with youth." Followed Jovarian from age fifteen. When the alliance with Nashim cracks and the Vexori are leaderless, he dons **Jovarian's red armour and crimson helm** to inspire them. Killed by Ashim in a duel the histories inflate to hours but which lasted **thirty seconds**. The lifted helm reveals a boy's face — the wound Ashim carries to Ophis.

### Kaelor — Blood-Son of Jovarian
Dream-cast inspiration: John Boyega. Senior Vexori champion. Wields a blade **forged from the ashes of Jovarian's ruined armour**. Challenges Ashim in Jovarian's name; killed by Ashim in **eleven minutes**. Dying prophecy: *"Not I, nor Jovarian, but the brother beside you shall end you. Remember the shadow that wears a familiar face."*

### Jamileighn — The Duchess
Dream-cast inspiration: Ruth Negga. Wife of Duke Oleric II; lover of Ishak. The "stupid, glittering dove" in Nashim's monologue — but canonically far more: she **knew the trap, warned Ishak, and watched him take it anyway.** Later aboard the **Ravager**; refuses the safe shuttle at every opportunity — "she will not be anyone's widow before the fact." Palette-adjacent to Ishak (violet/ash) but retains her own court colour.

### The Imperial Niece / The God-Emperor's Niece — UNNAMED (Lock 16)
Dream-cast inspiration: Taylor Russell. Niece of the God-Emperor, of the high priesthood; hair bound in the veil of untouched women; seized alive amid burning altars and carried in silver chains behind Jovarian's chariot as "insult before spoil." Later freed and crowned with wreaths of fire and gold when the temple is restored. **Never invent a name — caption her only as The Imperial Niece.**

### Kalia — Priestess / The Glass Eye
Dream-cast inspiration: Thuso Mbedu. Sister of the Sanctuary of Silence in Iseldora's High Temple. Grew up with Bashir in service of the Flame. Chooses "mercy over revelation." Crosses the Glass Wastes of Ophis with Bashir; records the Ashim-Lattice's account of the Sword; her chronicle is *The Chronicle of the Glass Eye* (hence Book Four's title). **She is the record.** Palette: archive blue, frankincense smoke, blue Iseldoran flame.

### Bashir — The Renegade
Dream-cast inspiration: Ashton Sanders. Former Vexori warrior-priest who defected ("I will serve truth above the throne"). Moves as a ghost through the Imperial Archives in a tattered Vexori prayer shawl; carries a sanctified blue-ember blade (the **Tooth of Silence**). Kills Grand Scribe Hiram and seals the deed with a **crescent-and-dagger** sigil. Later severs Ashim's hand with the same knife on Ophis: "Forgive me, Majesty." Ashim: "There is nothing to forgive." Counts lamps as his private ledger of cost.

### General Shadak — of the Black Death
Dream-cast inspiration: Delroy Lindo. Commander of the **Black Death** (small, elite, deniable Imperial strike teams via Janissary drop platforms). Stayed loyal to Ashim; prepared thoroughly for Laurentis's possible execution and is kept in command for exactly that honesty ("Men die for precision"). **The Witness** — placed beside Laurentis so the end has one set of uncorrupted eyes. Places the crown on Marcellus's head and thinks of everyone not in the room.

### Lord Verus Aureline — Master of Whispers
Dream-cast inspiration: Jeffrey Wright. Laurentis's spymaster. Steers suspicion toward the Church; charged by Laurentis to "find who is truly behind these Quiet Knives." Survives the Hall of Unity. Careful, grey, indispensable, never quite trusted.

### Duke Oleric II of Mandaria
Dream-cast inspiration: Mahershala Ali. Husband of Jamileighn; the cuckolded duke whose grief/surrender becomes Nashim's first *casus belli* ("I will ride the grief of Oleric"). Shapur sends his sons to accept Oleric's surrender — the errand that begins everything.

### Princess Meret Saba XXXIII — Pharaoh of Iseldora
Dream-cast inspiration: Lupita Nyong'o. Becomes Pharaoh in Laurentis's first year. Sends a single letter: **"We are watching."** Laurentis burns it. The Church-as-rival-power incarnate. (Note: later Pharaohs **Xyra VII** and High Priestess **Sephira** also appear — see §4.)

### Asha Kers — Queen of the Vah'Sumir
Dream-cast inspiration: Tati Gabrielle. Mother of Laurentis by Shapur. Of the Vah'Sumir. Named in the family tree and in Laurentis's memory ("Asha. Khuvius. Ashim."). Ashari'i-associated lineage.

### Fatima
Dream-cast inspiration: Angela Bassett. Mother of Ashim and Ishak by Shapur. Named in the locked family tree. Matriarch of the half-brother line.

### Admiral Harkor — of the Black Fleet
Dream-cast inspiration: David Oyelowo. Admiral of Ishak's Black Fleet. His treachery is visible to Ishak through the **crystal eye** from the beginning; Ishak keeps him alive because "the fleet needs an adhesive."

### Captain Aenius Rasmus de Los Andes — "Rasmus"
Dream-cast inspiration: Chiwetel Ejiofor. Chronicler-observer whose wry accounts frame Acts VIII–XI ("The empire did not collapse. Which annoyed everyone who had prepared speeches."). Liked Laurentis immediately, "which made his account suspicious."

### Baron Sarimus von Sah'wahr Viele of Tyrelis
Dream-cast inspiration: Giancarlo Esposito. Chronicler of the Last Emperors; supplies the book's epigraph. "Respected [Laurentis]. That was worse [than liking him]." The cold, precise historian who "did not like Laurentis" but recorded him faithfully.

### Kaisar Vane
Dream-cast inspiration: Andy Serkis. Late-saga "correction" figure. Arrives "as a pillar of fire"; installs the **Harmonic Lattice / Harmonic Correction** (the 7.83 Hz frequency that neutralises anxiety, ambition, grief and joy alike — "the people feel like stones in a wall"). Integrates into the Dragon Throne across five worlds. Does **not** die: withdraws into the archive, red eye dimmed to an ember — the "Stone God," tended by the Scribe.

### Sephira — High Priestess of Iseldora
(Not on the numbered dream-cast slate but canonically important.) Personal envoy of the Pharaoh; uses Kalia's genuine faith as cover for political calculation; offers Church Vexori guards and is politely refused. Dream-cast inspiration on the slate is Cate Blanchett listed as **Sephira** (slate #2). Style: white and amber robes.

## 4. FACTIONS & ORDERS

- **House Pierre von Care / the Dragon Throne** — the imperial bloodline; seat on Maldorus; legitimacy bound to relics and the Lattice. Colours: imperial gold, ivory, black, Solumine green. Heraldry: the Dragon Seal; the Imperial Phoenix (Laurentis's banner).
- **The Red Vexori** — frontier guardians turned eternal enemies after the Dymora Oath. Dried-blood-red armour, bronze scale, engineered for loyalty and violence. Distinct from imperial forces: rougher, sacral, oath-bound.
- **The Church of Iseldora** — female Pharaohs (Meret Saba XXXIII, Xyra VII) and High Priestesses (Sephira); warrior-priests; the blue ever-flame; Sanctuary of Silence. Rival power to the Throne. White, amber, blue flame.
- **The Quiet Knives** — Gamelon's three-tier leverage engine (see §5).
- **The Black Death** — Shadak's deniable elite strike teams; "the Emperor's final argument."
- **The Hapsburgi / Kasparian Belt** — Nashim's power base; caliphs and janissaries; Central-Asian/Persian-imperial register.
- **The Builders** — a technocratic guild that "recognises no coronation"; humbled by Goliath.
- **The Mandarians / minor Trumps / broken houses** — the dispossessed who swear to Ishak in the caverns of Gathis.
- **Marcellus's movement** — plain white flag, golden sunburst; the Humanist Manifesto; the Engine of Bone armada.
- **The Pre-Cursors (First Age / First Men)** — "not first, but early"; builders of both the Lattice and the Sword as opposed correction mechanisms.

## 5. THE QUIET KNIVES — THREE-TIER LEVERAGE ENGINE (Lock 14)

*"The Quiet Knives is not an assassination network. It is a leverage engine. An assassination network kills people. A leverage engine changes what people do."* — Gamelon's notebook (Record VIII).

- **Tier One — Information (14 individuals):** archivists/functionaries embedded in the palace administration reporting the court's "internal weather." None know they report to the same destination; each thinks they do a private favour for a clever jester.
- **Tier Two — Influence (7 individuals):** positioned to *shape* decisions — a Solumine physician adjusting the Emperor's clarity, a Lattice technician introducing comms delays, a senior scribe accelerating/delaying filings.
- **Tier Three — Resolution (3 operatives):** the last resort, deployed only when Tiers One and Two fail and only against civilisational-scale problems. Confirmed Tier-Three deployments: **Hiram** (archive of the Emperor's crimes), **Jaleel** (famine-relief diversion), **Oko** (Artemis III purge), and — the longest-delayed in the network's history — **Nashim**.

The network "was never meant to survive the coronation." Its success is defined as becoming unnecessary. Depict as candle amber / archive blue / ink black; notebooks, ledgers, corridors, lamps, hands.

## 7. ARTEFACTS, SHIPS & RELICS

- **The Sword of Iseldora** — Pre-Cursor "correction mechanism," not merely a weapon. Fuses to its bearer; grants power through gradual self-consumption ("it eats the certainty"). Forged by the First Age to correct the Lattice's order. Moves through the Fold without signature. Consumes Ishak into the Withered King. Shatters when Laurentis's spear passes through Ishak and the Ghost of Ashim.
- **The Lattice** — predictive AI network governing governance, trade, agriculture, military coordination. Pre-Cursor origin; Ashim merges with it on Ophis, "its prisoner and administrator simultaneously." Fractured by the Blue Signal; later crashed by the spear; replaced by Vane's Harmonic Lattice.
- **The Sun-Disc** — the God-Emperor's cult relic, "heavier than the shields of ten men," torn from the Mars altar by Jovarian.
- **The Black Crown of Cassian** — seven dark purple rubies; worn by Laurentis at his one-day coronation.
- **The Dragon Seal / Dragon Ring** — imperial biometric key; authenticates the Blue Signal.
- **The Iron Spear** — Laurentis's weapon; ends the Withered King, the Ghost, and the old order in one blow.
- **The Tooth of Silence** — Bashir's sanctified blue-ember dagger; kills Hiram, severs Ashim's hand.
- **The Blackened Staff** — Marcellus's grounding rod; disconnects the Harmonic Lattice, spreading a Dead Zone from the Great Plaza.
- **Solumine** — fluid that sustains Shapur past natural life; "maintains function without feeling."
- **The Engine of Bone** — Marcellus's patchwork armada (Hestian freighters + Bedouin scrapships).
- **Ferro-ceramic** — standard imperial armour material; lighter than steel, energy-resistant.
- **The Blue Signal / Third Dawn** — Ashim's biometric transmission from Ophis; fractures the Lattice globally; signals the end of the old order.

## 8. AMBIGUITIES CARRIED FROM VERSION 6 (do not resolve silently)

1. **Te Pes** (Lock 17): spelling and geography are not clarified in V6. The "Sack of Te Pes" is not narrated as a discrete scene in the supplied text; it is treated here as a mandated scene to be *constructed* consistent with the Vexori assault pattern (temple-city plunder, burning altars). Retain "Te Pes"; portray as an imperial palace-city / sacred complex under Vexori assault; record the ambiguity. **Do not omit.**
2. **The Imperial Niece** (Lock 16): unnamed. Never invent a name.
3. **Name spelling variants** in V6 to preserve as-is unless the author rules: *Valerien* (Records/tree) vs *Valerian* (Acts IX–XI narrative) — treat as the same person; the family tree's **Valerien** is the canonical spelling for the character's true name, **Valerian** appears as his public/officer name in the narrative. *Rasmus* is "Aenius Rasmus de Los Andes" (also rendered "Ramsus" once — typo; use **Rasmus**). *Jamileighn* spelling retained. *Ashari'i / Ashari'I* — capitalisation varies in source; standardise display as **Ashari'i**.
4. **Pharaoh identity across time:** Meret Saba XXXIII is Pharaoh in Laurentis's early reign; **Xyra VII** is Pharaoh during the Ashim-return act (decades later). Both are canonical; the 93-year span accommodates succession. Do not merge them.
5. **"Ancient Discipline" vs "Long Discipline":** the glossary notes "Ancient Discipline" as the formal in-military name for the same system called the **Long Discipline** elsewhere. Treat as synonyms; prefer **Long Discipline** in captions.
6. **Duel duration:** Record I deflates the Ashim–Nostavius duel to "thirty seconds" and frames the three-hour Ashim–Jovarian duel as the real one; the Song inflates both. For art, honour the Companion Record's correction in captions while the epic register may inform composition.
