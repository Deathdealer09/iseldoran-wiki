# QUIET KNIVES — IMAGE LEDGER (04)

*Asset register for **Quiet Knives: A Companion to the Code of Martyrs** (K. S. Pierre, PCM GenCon Group). Gate-2 production document. Every row derives from `01-canon-bible.md` (LOCKED) and the Version-6 manuscript; where those disagree, the Canon Bible wins, and where it disagrees with V6, V6 wins.*

**Nature of work:** Unofficial fan-casting / concept-art exercise. "Actor inspirations" denote *inspiration only* — original performers inspired by an actor's age, presence and register, never a claimed likeness. No performer has been contacted, approved, or attached.

**Standing rules applied to every row (do not restate per-row):**
- Clean generated art carries **NO embedded words, glyphs, seals-as-text, or captions.** All names, titles, captions, page numbers and heraldic legends are added later as Canva text layers (Status → Typography stage).
- War is **hot, physical, costly** (Lock 19). The Vexori are never effortless victims (Lock/QA). Shapur is never indifferent — his register is terrifying, controlled rage.
- The **Imperial Niece is never named** (Lock 16). The **Sack of Te Pes** is retained and never omitted (Lock 17); its staging is flagged **CONSTRUCTED** per §8 of the Canon Bible.
- Preserve every established spelling. No modern dress, no cyberpunk neon, no generic fantasy armour, correct faction heraldry, no duplicated people within a frame.
- Genealogy lock: Ashim/Ishak are Shapur's sons and Laurentis's **half-brothers** → Marcellus's **great-uncles**; never his siblings. Valerien is Marcellus's **father**.
