## Caption method (proven)
Per page: `insert_shape` a dark band (`#0B0B0D`, opacity 0.55) at bottom (top≈1776, h≈470; cover uses a top band), then `add_text`, then `format_text` (font 46, `#EAE3D2`, center). Cover title font 150 bold. Text is added as editable Canva layers over clean art (no text baked into generated images).
