# QUIET KNIVES — VISUAL-LANGUAGE & PALETTE BIBLE

*Gate 2 reference. Governs every generation prompt, every plate, every page. Derived from prompt §6 (Unified Visual Language) and the Canon Bible palettes.*

---

## 1. THE TARGET LOOK

The adaptation should read like a **US$300-million prestige science-fantasy production photographed for IMAX**:

- ultra-photorealistic large-format cinematic imagery; realistic anatomy and natural skin texture;
- physically plausible fabric, metal, stone, smoke, blood, dust, glass, crystal, and fire;
- monumental scale with emotionally legible faces;
- high dynamic range, controlled grain, detailed blacks;
- coherent **African, Central Asian, Mongolian, Persian, and future-imperial** influences — a single fused imperial culture, **not** a costume collage;
- **NO** cartoon style, generic cyberpunk, random neon, or generic fantasy design.

**Identity mandate:** the imperial line, Ashari'i-associated characters, and the central war factions carry a predominantly **Black, Afro-diasporic, Afro-Asian, or Afro-Mongolian** identity. Preserve distinct faces, complexions, ages, bodies, and regional character across every plate — never a single repeated face or physique.

## 3. SEQUENCE ACCENTS (per faction / arc)

Each strand of the book carries a recognisable accent chord so a reader can feel *where* they are without a caption:

- **Jovarian / Red Vexori:** rust, black, bronze, blood red.
- **Shapur's court:** gold, ivory, black, Solumine green.
- **Ishak & the Sword:** violet, ash, dead silver.
- **Ashim & the Lattice:** black adamant, cobalt, spectral blue.
- **The Quiet Knives:** candle amber, archive blue, ink black.
- **Marcellus:** earth, rain, iron, seed green.

Additional working chords (consistent with canon):
- **Church of Iseldora:** bone white, amber, blue flame.
- **Nashim / Hapsburgi–Kasparian:** oiled bronze, jasmine cream, copper, decadent gold.
- **Laurentis / Stone Giant:** forged-iron black, cobalt, storm-glass grey, rain.
- **Kaisar Vane / Harmonic Correction:** sterile white, ember red, still grey (the colour of neutralised feeling).

## 4. TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM (applied as Canva text layers only — never inside generated art)

- **Display / titles:** monumental inscriptional serif (Roman-capital, carved-in-stone character) for part titles, plate titles, the wordmark. True small caps where the face provides them.
- **Body text:** classical serif with generous leading for the full manuscript (long-form readability at 11×14 or 12×12).
- **Captions:** restrained sans serif, small, for scene captions, sources, and continuity notes.
- **Nameplates (portraits):** display serif for the character name; a lighter weight for the actor line; italic small caps for "Unofficial fan-casting concept."
- **Folios & running heads:** consistent throughout; part/record name in the running head, page number in the outer margin.

**Nameplate lockup (Canva text layers, added after clean art):**
```
CHARACTER NAME
Dream-cast actor: ACTOR NAME
Unofficial fan-casting concept
```

## 5. CONTINUITY LAW

Consistency is never assumed from a repeated name. Every new image is compared against the **approved character plate** and the **approved architecture plate** before it is accepted.

Locked per recurring character (build a reference plate first): facial structure, complexion, eyes, hair, height/build, scars, jewellery, costume, armour, heraldry, age, and transformation stage.

Tracked across linked scenes:
- generational ageing across the 93 years (see chronology §D);
- Ishak's physical consumption by the Sword (progressive, one-directional);
- Ashim's progression into the Lattice (champion → sleepless → sapphire-eyed machine-god);
- Valerien's continuity as Gamelon (same bone structure beneath the paint);
- wounds, blood, dust, and clothing damage carried *between* linked scenes (a wound in scene N is present in scene N+1);
- architectural lineages for Maldorus, Miraflores Palace, the Dragon Throne, the God-Emperor's Temple, Dymora, Ophis III, Kasparia, the Ravager, the Black Fleet, Vexori camps, archives, sacred vaults, ruined cities, and Te Pes;
- **distinct** imperial, Vexori, Iseldoran, Hapsburgi, Ashari'i, and Pre-Cursor design languages — never interchangeable.

**No generic armies. No repeated battle composition. No repeated palace design.**

## 6. GENERATION-PROMPT TEMPLATE (every image uses all ten fields)

1. exact characters + continuity stage;
2. age, complexion, facial structure, hair, build, scars, injuries;
3. costume, armour, heraldry, weapons, props;
4. location & architecture;
5. action & emotional objective;
6. camera height, framing, lens character, depth, composition;
7. light source, weather, atmosphere, palette, materials;
8. scale & background activity;
9. aspect ratio & intended page use;
10. **negative constraints.**

**Standing negative constraints (append to every prompt):** no embedded words/letters/captions/seals inside the art; no malformed hands or anatomy; no duplicated or cloned people; no plastic/wax skin; no floating objects; no incoherent weapons; no incorrect heraldry; no modern clothing, zippers, or eyewear; no random neon or cyberpunk; no generic fantasy armour; no cartoon/anime rendering; no lens flare gimmickry; no watermark.

---

## 7. LIKENESS & POLICY GUARDRAIL

If image generation refuses or cannot accurately reproduce a real actor's likeness, **comply with the policy.** Create an **original performer** inspired by the actor's age range, presence, energy, and dramatic register — never a claimed exact likeness. Record every such substitution in the casting dossier. Never attempt to evade a platform safeguard. Nothing in this book is casting news.

---

## 8. ASPECT-RATIO CONVENTIONS

- Full-page portraits & environment plates: **4:5 / 5:7 portrait** (to the chosen trim).
- Double-page battle & panorama spreads: **~2:1 landscape**.
- Diagrams/maps/timelines: sized to spread, landscape.
- Covers: to trim + full bleed + spine allowance.
- Detail crops / vignettes: square or as needed (do not count toward the 105 quota).
