# QUIET KNIVES — CHRONOLOGY & THE NINETY-THREE-YEAR COLLAPSE

*Source of truth: Version 6, especially **Record XI · The Century Between**, Appendix A synopses, and the locked family tree. The collapse spans **93 years, 4 months, 11 days** — "between the night Ishak took the Sword from the Vault and the night the Black Fleet broke atmosphere over Maldorus." Art must show visible generational change across this span (Canon Lock 7).*

---

## A. THE TWO TIME REGISTERS

The saga braids two clocks the book must keep distinct:

1. **The Epic Register (mythic time).** The Song of Jovarian, the Vexori War acts, the Pursuit of Ishak — narrated in Homeric cadence, compressing months into stanzas. Precedes the 93-year count; it is the *deep past* whose debts (the Dymora Oath) come due later.
2. **The Administrative Register (Year 0–93).** Record XI's precise ledger, dated from the theft of the Sword. This is the spine of the collapse.

**Ordering note:** The Jovarian cycle and the Vexori War (Ashim kills Nostavius, Kaelor, Jovarian; the Dymora Oath) belong to the deep past — a century-scale prelude that establishes the Vexori hatred. The theft of the Sword at the Feast of Masks is **Year 0**, the first domino of the *dated* collapse. Ashim's pursuit, failure, and departure for Ophis all occur in Year 0.

## C. THE DATED COLLAPSE (Year 0 → Year 93)

Drawn verbatim in structure from **Record XI · The Century Between**.

| Year | Events |
|---|---|
| **Year 0** | The **Feast of Masks**. Ishak (age 23) steals the Sword of Iseldora from the gene-locked Vault and flees with Jamileighn; the Sword fuses to his flesh at once. Ashim pursues across the outer systems and fails. Ashim returns to the empty scabbard and Shapur's public judgment: *"you are only the man I send to look for it."* Three weeks later Ashim departs alone, no flight plan, for **Ophis III**. He does not return as a man. |
| **Years 1–6** | **Shapur's final years.** He directs Gamelon through the Lattice; watches Ishak's fleet accrete among the dispossessed; watches Laurentis hold the Rim; does not summon Laurentis back ("vanity in a man who knows it is vanity"). Discovers, privately, that the empire manages *without* the Sword. |
| **Year 6** | **Shapur XIV dies** in the Dragon Throne, during a morning briefing, between two administrative reports. The Solumine systems flag a cardiac anomaly that "resolves incorrectly." No ceremony. |
| **Years 6–12** | **The Interregnum.** The court argues succession. Laurentis takes three weeks to decide (long enough to secure the Rim), returns, is crowned (the one-day coronation), and rules his first year. |
| **Years 12–40** | **The Long Governance.** Laurentis rules; the empire stabilises; the outer worlds survive but do not thrive. The Quiet Knives operates in the background. The **Ashim rumours** begin ~**Year 28**, when the Blue Signal is first detected by independent Rim sensors; Laurentis classifies the reports. |
| **Years 40–70** | **Marcellus's youth, formation, and departure.** He leaves the palace at **age 30**, going first to Hestia, then the outer worlds, "learning their names." |
| **Years 70–93** | **The Rebellion.** Marcellus builds his insurgency; Laurentis manages it; the Quiet Knives operates on *both* sides simultaneously (unknown to either). |
| **Year 89** | **Valerian (Valerien), Laurentis's son, Marcellus's father, is killed.** (The Tower in the Rain: Laurentis learns of it alone; the city sings Ashim's name below.) |
| **Year 91** | **The parley in the Hall of Unity fails** — not by the Bannon incident (a catalyst) but by the collision of two correct arguments: order vs dignity. |
| **Year 92** | **The Kalia–Bashir mission to Ophis succeeds.** The Third Dawn. Ashim fires the **Blue Signal**, fracturing the Lattice globally. |
| **Year 93** | **The Black Fleet arrives.** The siege of Maldorus. Laurentis drives the Iron Spear through Ishak and the Ghost of Ashim simultaneously; the Sword shatters and the Lattice crashes. The old order ends. |

**Post-Year-93 (undated, Code of Martyrs Books III–IV):** Maldorus in ruins; the Lattice shattered to 12% coherence; **Kaisar Vane** arrives and installs the **Harmonic Correction** across five worlds; Marcellus descends with the **Engine of Bone**, grounds the Harmonic Lattice with the **Blackened Staff**, and is crowned in the obsidian hall by Gamelon's dying hands — **the Coronation of the Mud.** Vane withdraws into the archive (does not die). First words of the new reign: *"We have a lot of work to do."*
