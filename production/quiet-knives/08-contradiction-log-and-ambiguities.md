## B. UNRESOLVED-AMBIGUITIES REPORT (one page, for the back matter)

*The following points are genuinely unresolved in Version 6. They are recorded rather than silently "fixed." Where a scene had to proceed, the chosen staging is marked **[constructed]** and does not claim to be quoted canon.*

1. **Te Pes — spelling and geography.** The place-name's spelling and location are not established in the supplied manuscript, and no discrete "Sack of Te Pes" episode is narrated. The scene is retained as mandated and rendered as an imperial palace-city / sacred complex under Vexori assault. **[constructed]**

2. **The Imperial Niece — identity.** She is never named. She is depicted and captioned only as "The Imperial Niece / The God-Emperor's Niece." Her ultimate life beyond being freed and crowned at the temple's restoration is not detailed.

3. **The Tell Maiden.** The woman over whom Jovarian and Nashim quarrel is unnamed and appears only as a plot catalyst; she is depicted but not named.

4. **Valerien / Valerian and the "brother/uncle" line.** The exact wording of Gamelon's dying revelation differs between passages (see C1). The father→son fact is fixed; the spoken line's precise form is treated per the correction the manuscript lands on.

5. **Discharge of the Dymora Oath.** Book Four states the oath sworn over Jovarian's body "is never formally discharged in the saga… Marcellus inherits it. What he does with it is not recorded." Left open.

6. **Kaisar Vane's fate.** He "does not die" but "withdraws into the archive," red eye dimmed to an ember. His future is deliberately unresolved.

7. **Pharaoh succession dates.** The precise years separating Meret Saba XXXIII, Xyra VII, and Sephira's tenure are not given, only that they span the 93 years.

8. **Exact duel choreography vs. stated duration.** The Songs and the Companion Records give different durations for the same duels (see C4); captions follow the Records, composition may follow the Songs.

*All eight are surfaced in the book's Unresolved-Ambiguities back-matter page so that no reader mistakes a constructed or open point for settled canon.*
