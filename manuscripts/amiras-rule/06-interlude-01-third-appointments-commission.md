# Interlude 1 — Record of the Third Appointments Commission

I include the following for what it would later make plain, though it shows nothing on its face. At the time of its filing no one in the empire read it twice. I have copied it whole, header and seal, so that the reader may pass over the same line I once passed over, and feel afterward the weight of having done so.

---

BUREAU OF ESTABLISHMENTS
OFFICE OF THE OUTER PREFECTURES
RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS, THIRD APPOINTMENTS COMMISSION
Session opened the fourteenth day, third quarter, under imperial administrative authority. Filed New Terra. Folio 412-C, established series.

PRESENT: the convening registrar; two assessors of the Bureau of Establishments; one clerk of record; deputed observers from the prefectural offices of Sennar, Adwa, Lomé, and the Lower Cataracts, attending without vote.

PURPOSE OF SESSION: to review, confirm, and enter into the standing imperial record those judicial and administrative appointments across the Outer Prefectures referred to this Commission since the close of the prior session, and to reconcile such appointments as remain unentered from prior authority.

---

ITEM ONE. The registrar opened the session and confirmed the quorum. The clerk read the standing remit of the Commission: that it holds no power to create appointments, only to review those lawfully made and to enter them into the record where review finds them in order. The remit was read, noted, and entered without objection.

ITEM TWO. The assessors presented the schedule of appointments referred since the prior session, numbering one hundred and sixteen across the four prefectures named above. Of these, ninety-one were supported by complete documentation, reviewed individually, and confirmed. The clerk entered each by name, office, prefecture, and date of lawful appointment. The schedule of the ninety-one is appended as Annex A and forms part of this record.

ITEM THREE. Of the remaining twenty-five appointments, the assessors reported that documentation was incomplete at the time of referral. The registrar directed that these be held over and that the originating offices be instructed to supply the missing instruments before the close of the present session. Nineteen were so completed and confirmed. They are appended as Annex B.

ITEM FOUR. Six appointments remained unsupported by complete documentation at the close of the supplementary period. The assessors raised the standing question of backlog, observing that a number of appointments confirmed under the authority of the prior Commission had never been entered into the current record owing to the volume of referrals, and that the affected offices had for some time operated under appointments that were lawful but unrecorded, to the confusion of the prefectural registries.

The registrar reminded the Commission of the carry-forward reconciliation provision, established under the founding instrument of this body and exercised at each prior session, which permits an appointment confirmed under a prior Commission's authority to be entered into the current record without fresh review, upon production of the prior confirmation and on the attestation of the originating office that the appointment continues in force. The provision exists to reconcile backlog and to spare the Commission the labor of reviewing afresh that which a prior Commission has already found in order.

The assessors confirmed that each of the six appointments in question bore a prior confirmation and an attestation from its originating office. The registrar directed that the six be entered under the carry-forward reconciliation, citing in each entry the prior Commission's authority and the date of original confirmation rather than the date of present entry, as the provision requires. The six are appended as Annex C. Of these, four originate in the Sennar Prefecture.

ITEM FIVE. The clerk recorded that the total of one hundred and sixteen appointments referred to this session had been disposed of: one hundred and ten confirmed on review, six entered under carry-forward reconciliation. The registrar noted that this disposition cleared the standing backlog for the prefectures named and directed that the originating offices be so informed.

ITEM SIX. The deputed observer for the Sennar Prefecture entered a query as to whether the carry-forward entries under Annex C would be subject to review at a future session. The registrar answered that an appointment entered under the reconciliation provision is, by the terms of that provision, already confirmed, and is not referred forward for further review except upon a contest lawfully raised through the prefectural courts. The query and the answer were entered without further discussion.

ITEM SEVEN. There being no further business, the registrar moved that the record of the session be closed, the annexes appended, and the whole transmitted to the standing imperial record under the seal of the Bureau. The motion carried. The clerk read back the disposition of all one hundred and sixteen appointments, and the record was found correct as read.

The session was closed by the registrar at the appointed hour.

---

Entered and attested by the clerk of record. Reviewed by the convening registrar. Transmitted to the standing imperial record, New Terra, folio 412-C.

SEALED UNDER THE AUTHORITY OF THE BUREAU OF ESTABLISHMENTS, OFFICE OF THE OUTER PREFECTURES, THIRD APPOINTMENTS COMMISSION CONVENED.
