## Book I — *The Forging* (recommended debut)

### Amazon description (primary)

**The empire didn't raise a princess. It forged a weapon.**

Across five frontier wars, Khuvius Pierre von Care — Crown Prince of War — and the eleven commanders called the Star Wolves bleed to hold a dying rim together. The sixth war, Hesh-Kar, doesn't just cost them. It teaches them what the empire truly needs: not another general, but a weapon that will never break.

So they make one.

*The Forging* is the birth and making of **Asha Kers I** — from the Orbital Battle of Tarrid and the Khotai Pass to the Council of Three, the summoning of Nayra of Kithoun, and the nineteen years that turn a child into the God-Empress who will remake a universe. Some are forged by love. Some by war. Asha is forged by both, by the men who would die for her and the empire that would spend her.

Imperial science fiction with the weight of real history — for readers of *Dune*, *Foundation*, and *Warhammer 40,000*.

**Before the God-Empress, there were the wolves who cleared her path.**

Long before Asha Kers I, Crown Prince Khuvius Pierre von Care fights the wars that build an empire's doctrine — the Arexus Corridor, the Karsai Belt, the Vah'Sumir skirmish, the Leviathan of New Terra.
