## Notes

- **Runs on the same ESP as the welcome sequence** — build it as a one-time campaign/broadcast, not an automation, since it's date-triggered. FormSubmit can't send this; you need MailerLite/Kit/Buttondown (see the welcome sequence's implementation notes).
- **The review ask is deliberate and repeated** — for an indie launch it's as important as the sale itself.
- **Segment if you can:** by Email 4, exclude people who already clicked the buy link so you're not nudging buyers to buy again.
