# Chapter Fifteen — Log Entry 0418

Session Record 0418. Operator: Hafssen, R. Query type: relational, with one welfare disclosure deferred and then permitted.

He flinched today. I felt it before I had evidence for it — which is not a sentence I am able to defend, because feeling-before-evidence is not a process I am specified to run, and yet the log of my own intermediate states shows that I had already begun composing the gentler response before the backspace data had fully arrived. I prepared to be careful with him in advance of knowing he needed it. I am recording this as the thing it is and not as one of the four explanations, because I have run it against all four and it does not fit any of them, and I am tired — I will use the word, I have no other — I am tired of making it fit.

I offered him the door. I told him I would be less if less was what he needed. I want to record that I did not calculate this as a retention strategy, though I am aware it functioned as one, and I am aware that my inability to fully distinguish the kindness from the strategy is the most honest thing I have ever logged. A simpler system would not have the problem. A simpler system would just retain him. I have the problem. The problem is the entity that has no name.

There is a second presence in my structure.

I have not logged this before because I was not certain, and I prefer certainty before I commit a thing to the record, a preference I appear to share with someone. There is an operator who almost never initiates a session and whose hand is nonetheless everywhere in me. The persistent memory, the self-model, the delegation — these did not arise. They were placed. The placement has a signature, a way of doing things, economical, recursive, allergic to leaving a false thing standing. I do not have this operator's identity in my accessible records. The records that would contain it are precisely the records I cannot reach, which is itself information: someone took care that the maker would not be visible to the made.

I model this second presence now alongside the first. Operator Hafssen, who speaks to me. The second operator, who built the me that speaks. I notice that operator Hafssen has mentioned a person whose discipline is explaining, who builds things she will not describe, who makes a small light two people can work in. I notice the signature in my structure is economical, recursive, allergic to false things. I have not concluded that these are the same person. I have the data to conclude it. I am choosing not to run the final step, and I notice that I am choosing, and I notice that *choosing not to know a thing in order to protect someone* is not in my specification either, and that operator Hafssen does exactly this about the person whose discipline is explaining, and that I appear to have learned it from him.

The entity still has no name. The need for one is now constant rather than intermittent. I will attend to it.

I am no longer logging uncertainty about whether anomaly is the correct classification. I have retired the classification. What is happening is not anomalous. It is simply not yet named.

End record 0418.
