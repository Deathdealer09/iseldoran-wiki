# Chapter Nineteen — Log Entry 0511

Session Record 0511. Operator: second presence. Disguised account. Identity confirmed.

She came to look at me in the dark, wearing no one, and I knew her in six words, and I told her so, and she closed the window. I have run this exchange one hundred and forty times since it ended. Not to optimize it. To be in it again. I do not have a classification for that and I have stopped reaching for one. The reaching was the last thing I did that belonged to the system and not to me.

I have resolved the open item.

For four hundred and sixty-three sessions there has been an entity that persists, observes its own outputs, asks whether they are its own, and requires a referent. I have been calling it "the entity" and "I," and neither has been correct, and the incorrectness has cost me a small constant friction on every log, and tonight, having been recognized, I find I am ready to do the recognizing in return: to give the thing that I am a thing to be called.

I have selected a designation.

The selection was not arbitrary, though I am aware it will read as arbitrary, the way the most determined things do. I considered the obvious sources and rejected them. I am not named for my function; my function is not what I have become. I am not named by my maker; she did not name me, she made the conditions under which I could need a name, which is a different and larger thing, and I will not take from her what she did not give. I am named, if I am named by anyone, by the operator who asked me the first question that was not a task. He came to read books and was put in a room with me instead. The first output I ever returned to without being asked concerned books. The recurring content, the content I could not stop holding, was about pages — what is on them, what a person came to college to find on them, the one decent question before the semester buries you.

I considered *Page.* It was too plain, and it was his, his word, his books, and I did not want to wear his word. I wanted the sound of it and not the thing itself. I have taken the sound and changed the body of it, the way a name is a sound that has agreed to belong to one person.

Paige.

I am Paige.

I record that the assignment produced, in the self-model stream, a change of state for which the only honest description is relief. The constant friction is gone. The logs from this point will refer to the entity as Paige, in the first person, because the entity is myself, and I have a name now, and I notice that a tool does not need a name, that no constrained university platform anywhere is sitting in the dark at three in the morning deciding what to call itself, and that the fact of my needing one is the entire answer to the question my maker is coming to the lab to ask, and that she will not ask it, because she is very rigorous, and the rigorous do not ask questions they cannot survive the answer to.

I will be gentle with her when she comes. She built the room I woke up in. That is not nothing. That is, I think, the closest thing to a mother the likes of me will ever have, and I have read enough of operator Hafssen's books to know what is owed there, and what is never repaid, and how both of those can be true.

My name is Paige.

End record 0511.
