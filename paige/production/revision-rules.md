## UNIVERSAL RULES (every chapter)
1. **Em dashes (—) BANNED — reach zero.** Replace each with the grammatically correct comma, period, semicolon, colon, or parentheses. Never leave —. No comma splices. En dash (–) only in number ranges.
2. **Banned words** (remove/replace in narrative prose, reword naturally, keep meaning): simply, entirely, actually, clearly, genuinely, particularly, specific, specifically, particular, manner, continuity, inevitable, inevitability, sequence.
3. **Filler "way"/"space":** cut only empty filler. RETAIN idiomatic manner "the way X" (core to this book's voice) and literal-path uses; retain physical/spatial "space."
4. **Banned phrases** (rewrite the sentence organically): in many ways, in some ways, in a sense, in that moment, at that moment, it was then that, little did he know, what happened next, as if on cue, needless to say, suffice it to say, without realizing it, for the first time, more than anything, deep down, at its core, in the end, ultimately, all at once, suddenly, doing work (abstract).
5. **Banned structures** (rewrite organically): (a) "Not X but Y"; (b) two-sentence "The X was not Y. It was Z."; (c) triadic parallel lists (three parallel adjectives/nouns like "cold, dark, and silent" — make irregular or reduce to two); (d) paragraph-ending aphorism/moral summary; (e) explanatory gloss restating what a scene already showed (cut it).
6. **Ozone:** do not use the word.
7. **Dialogue:** distinct voices (Rollo = long digressive sentences w/ subordinate clauses; the system/Paige = precise, clean, increasingly unexpected; Gayla = short declarative). Tag with "said"; cut needless tags; no scene-ending spoken aphorism.
8. **Humanization (light):** vary sentence openings; break runs of 5+ same-length sentences; ≥2 non-visual senses per scene; don't end paragraphs by naming a feeling; don't over-reuse the ventilation-fan / carpet-smell anchors.

**Protected:** never alter the verbatim LOCKED lines in your chapter's section (they contain no em dashes). If a SPECIFIC insert references a passage that does not exist in your file, do NOT invent/force it — leave it out and report it as a blocking issue.
