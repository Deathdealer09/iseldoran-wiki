THE ISELDORAN SAGAS

Official Master Archive & Interactive Lore Repository

The Iseldoran Wiki is the central digital archive for the Iseldoran Sagas — a vast science-fantasy universe spanning empires, god-kings, dynastic wars, cosmic theology, military orders, engineered civilizations, and the rise of the Pierre von Care bloodline from planetary rulers to universal sovereigns.

This repository serves as:

* A searchable lore compendium
* A historical archive of canon events
* A manuscript and development vault
* A visual and dynastic reference system
* A publishing and continuity tool for the Iseldoran universe

⸻

Overview

The Iseldoran Sagas combine:

* Imperial science fiction
* Dynastic political drama
* Mythic space opera
* Military realism
* Theological conflict
* African, Arabian, Mongolian, and imperial European influences

The setting spans tens of thousands of years and follows the evolution of humanity from fractured planetary civilizations into the Universal Empire ruled beneath the Dragon Throne.

Core themes include:

* The burden of rulership
* Bloodline as political infrastructure
* Empire as a survival mechanism
* The conflict between faith and state power
* Divine kingship versus human weakness
* The transformation of humanity through war and technology

Major Dynasties & Factions

The Pierre von Care Dynasty

The ruling imperial bloodline founded by:

* Eustace Bartholamer Pierre von Care
* Kerron Pierre von Care
* Cassian I, the first God-King

The dynasty later expands into thousands of years of imperial rule under the Dragon Throne.

⸻

The Church of Iseldora

A powerful religious institution led by female Pharaohs whose authority often rivals that of emperors themselves.

⸻

The Black Death

An independent elite order founded by Kaelen Rainmaker.

Not an army.
A precision force.

Used when entire civilizations fail.

⸻

The Sardukar & Red Sardukar

The shock legions and elite military academies of the empire.

⸻

The Vah’Sumir & Ashari’i

Genetically altered post-human civilizations engineered for survival and warfare on an existential scale.

⸻

The Hapsburgi

A dynastic imperial-caliphal house intertwined with the Pierre von Care line through blood, conquest, and theology.

Key Characters

Kerron Pierre von Care

The Dragon Emperor who conquered Trinidad, Venezuela, and eventually the Americas, founding the Trinitarian Empire.

Cassian I

The first true God-King who executed 433 rival princes during his coronation and transformed empire into divinity.

Niccolò Kerron von Hapsburgi — Thunderborn

A military prodigy raised within the Sardukar Écoles who became one of the deadliest commanders in imperial history.  

Khutun Ghegha Khan

Queen of the Belt and one of the most politically important figures in the imperial frontier wars.  

Lucius Luceron II

The “Soldier Emperor,” known for reform, restraint, and transforming the empire through administration rather than spectacle.  

Asha Kers I

The God-Empress known as La Diosa, creator of the Ashari’i and codifier of the Universal Khanate Law.

Canon Structure

The repository follows strict continuity rules.

Events, bloodlines, imperial succession, and military chronology are treated as historical record rather than loose mythology.

The current canonical imperial chronology extends from:

* Eustace I Bartholamer Pierre von Care
    through
* Mettenik I Pierre von Care

covering more than 35,000 years of imperial history.
